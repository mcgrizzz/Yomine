# JMdict lexicon data attribution

`jmdict-lexicon.bin` is adapted from JMdict (EDRDG / James William Breen). This
adapted data is distributed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Source notice: https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html

Changes: kept the written and kana forms of expression, adverb, conjunction and
particle entries, and the entry numbers of spellings an entry reads more than one
way; compiled a sorted binary index. No definitions are bundled.
Exact versions, source URLs and checksums are in `jmdict-lexicon.json`.
The reproducible generator is `research/lexical/compile_lexicon.py`.
